# Troubleshooting Log

Problems I ran into while building the homelab and how I fixed them.

### Problem: Family phones wouldn't join the tailnet

- **Symptom:** Family members installed the Tailscale app but couldn't get their phones registered and connected.
- **Cause:** New users on a tailnet need setup on the admin side too, not just on the phone.
- **Fix:** As the tailnet admin, I finished the setup from the admin console by adding their information so their devices could connect.
- **Lesson:** When onboarding non-technical users, handle the admin side first and walk them through the app in person. Write the steps down for next time.
